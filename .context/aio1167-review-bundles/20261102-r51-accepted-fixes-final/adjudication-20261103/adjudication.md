Correction: durably materialize explicit document-trash evidence even when local membership is absent; retain bounded page/queue materialization, ordered observations, sink acknowledgment and replay. Let the existing claim owner decide whether a claim exists. `gdrive_sync.py:3640` already settles an explicit `trashed: true` removal without a probe; lines 3470–3484 use fenced reconciliation and acknowledge only after success. Preserve those properties, partial completeness and restrictions.
