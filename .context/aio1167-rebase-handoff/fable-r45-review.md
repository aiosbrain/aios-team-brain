## Findings

**H1. HIGH, product bug (traced by me).** The C6 Retry-After hold is lost in the baseline phase.
`ingestion/aios_ingest/gdrive_sync.py:2392-2400` raises `_ProviderAdmissionClosed` from `_drain_pending`; `_finish_materialized_pages` calls it at `:2157/:2165`; `_enumerate_baseline` (`:1906`) has no handler; the caller's chain at `:1487-1510` is `except BrainError`, `except ProviderDeferred`, then bare `except Exception`, and `_ProviderAdmissionClosed` subclasses plain `Exception` (`:130`). Trigger: a baselining stream, first metadata or body read answers 429 with a Retry-After. Expected: every stream of the account gets `retry_not_before`, no further provider call this run, category `rate_limited`. Actual: checkpointed as `phase=partial, last_error="baseline: _ProviderAdmissionClosed"`, counted `provider_read`, the next stream runs against Google immediately, and the next poll retries at once. The C6 tests call `_drain_pending` directly and never pass through `_enumerate_baseline`, which is why they are green. Regression: drive `_run_gdrive_stream_unlocked` with a baselining stream and a `_metadata` stub raising `ProviderDeferred(category="rate_limited")`; assert both streams carry `retry_not_before`, `failure_categories == {"rate_limited": 1}` and no `provider_read`.

**H2. HIGH conditional, deployment hazard (agent C, trigger traced by me).** Migration `20260922190000_identity_snapshot_fence.sql:36-38` inserts every existing team as `repair_status='pending'`; `lib/access/authorization-epoch.ts:13-19` throws `AttributionRepairPendingError` until it is `complete`, which is the first await in the timeline cache, arc fusion, events and recompute routes (all 503). On production the repair scheduler drains it at boot. On any runtime where `register()` returns before the schedulers (copied-staging runtime policy, or `ATTRIBUTION_REPAIR_POLL_ENABLED=false` with `INGEST_POLL_ENABLED=false`), nothing drains it and Timeline, Pulse, arcs and events are 503 for every team until an admin clicks "Re-attribute content" per team. A team whose batch fails deterministically retries hourly forever with no terminal state. Whether the Railway staging environment hits the suppressed branch is an environment fact, not in the repo. Regression: dm test loading the migration over N teams with both poll flags off, asserting `authorizationEpoch` still throws after the idle interval, plus a runbook line for the manual step.

**M3.
