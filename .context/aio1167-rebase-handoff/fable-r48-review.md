## Year-9999 sentinel against the adjudication

The adjudication allows "a fail-closed, actionable representation/refusal" for a syntactically valid but unrepresentable deadline, and forbids reading it as permission to retry early. The sentinel satisfies fail-closed. `LATEST_INSTANT` at `sources/gdrive.py:88` serializes to a valid ISO instant, `_retry_deferred` at `gdrive_sync.py:3034-3047` compares without arithmetic, `hold_connection` at `state.py:1238` keeps it monotonically, `_fail_work` takes a max without addition, and no code adds a duration to a stored deadline. The brain stores progress as opaque JSON, so nothing on that side parses the instant. Unrepresentable classification covers both over-long delay-seconds and dates past year 9999, and the raise at `sources/gdrive.py:731-739` happens before any sleep or retry regardless of remaining attempts, which the unit tests confirm. Valid long delays, including the 99999999 case, are honored exactly and never capped. The weaknesses are the ones in F4 and F5: the representation is actionable only in the originating run, and it is identical to a literal year-9999 date. Both are consistent with the deferred override policy rather than violations of it.
